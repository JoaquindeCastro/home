About

An empowered generation of innovators with the wisdom and will to take on large-scale issues.

Based in the Philippines, we work with companies and institutions to organize programs that invite talented students to tackle issues of national and global significance.

01

Youth

Our programs aim to instill crucial critical thinking and technical skills in the youth as well as connect them with industry stakeholders to turn their ideas into reality.

02

Innovation

We help create solutions that address pressing problems and go beyond textbook thinking while ensuring they contribute to equitable growth and social benefit.

03

Sustainability

From smart cities to eco-tourism, sustainability remains a core theme of what we do. What better way to protect future generations than by empowering them to solve tomorrow's challenges?

Partners

Organizations we have worked with.

ULI Philippines

The Urban Land Institute Philippines is the national chapter of an international network of real estate professionals, leaders, and established authorities on the built environment. ULI powered the NOVUS Case Competition.

TAJARA

TAJARA Leisure and Hospitality Group is a leading hospitality consulting and development company specializing in hotel, residential, and tourism development. TAJARA Hospitality co-organized the ENVision Case Competition.

AIM

The Asian Institute of Management is a pioneer in management education and home to the country's fastest growing super computer. AIM was the venue of the ENVision Case Competition.
